Organized & Held in Kitigan Zibi Anishinabeg

This raffle is organized and held in Kitigan Zibi Anishinabeg. Funds raised will support the development of Restoring Algonquin Presence and its work to create infrastructure, sales opportunities and access to larger markets for Algonquin entrepreneurs.

What Are We Building?

Restoring Algonquin Presence is about creating practical opportunities for Algonquin entrepreneurs to bring their businesses and ideas into high-traffic Ottawa markets.

That includes carts and professional table setups, access to markets, advertising and promotion, sales training and hands-on support, and opportunities to test ideas without taking on the usual costs of entering a major market.
