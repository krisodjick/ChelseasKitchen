About the gathering

Forest Light, Forest Wisdom

Hosted by the School of Never-Ending Learning, this three-hour workshop features a screening of Elder Albert Dumont’s film Forest Light, Forest Wisdom, a conversation about Algonquin teachings, and an art-making workshop inspired by the experience.

Workshop registration is managed separately through Eventbrite. Ordering food below does not register you for the workshop.
