More than acknowledgement

Support actual Algonquin presence.

Every September, organizations across Ottawa talk about reconciliation. Land acknowledgements are read. Orange shirts are worn. Important conversations happen.

We're asking people to add something very simple to that: buy lunch from an Algonquin-owned business.

Whether you're ordering for yourself, two coworkers or a small office group, your order directly supports Chelsea's Kitchen and the work we're building toward for 2027.

Why are we doing this? Because reconciliation should be about more than acknowledging Algonquin territory. We want to help restore visible, everyday Algonquin presence in Ottawa through food, entrepreneurship and the mobile cart project we are building for 2027.
