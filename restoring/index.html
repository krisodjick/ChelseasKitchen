Why RAP exists

A good idea needs
a place to grow.

Algonquin communities are full of people with ideas, skills and businesses. Reaching a larger market takes more than a good product: it takes space, visibility, confidence and access to customers.

Restoring Algonquin Presence is being developed to connect those entrepreneurs with opportunities in Ottawa, through shared sales carts, events, promotion and practical support.

Presence in everyday life.

Ottawa is on unceded Algonquin territory. RAP aims to make Algonquin participation visible in the city’s daily economic life: people working, creating, selling and building their own businesses.

The 2027 pilot

Three carts.
Many possibilities.

A proposed three-cart pilot will bring food, products and changing experiences together. Locations, financing and operating arrangements are being developed ahead of the planned spring 2027 launch.

For Algonquin entrepreneurs

Bring your idea.
Build your confidence.

The goal is to provide sales opportunities, training and hands-on support at no cost to participating entrepreneurs. RAP is working to secure the funding and partnerships needed to make this possible.

Practical sales training

Kris Odjick brings 20 years of sales experience and experience delivering sales training across the country. Support will focus on approaching customers, explaining a product, setting prices, closing a sale and learning what works.

Your business stays yours

Entrepreneurs bring their own products and ideas. RAP provides a platform to reach customers and develop sales skills while participants keep their own business identity.
