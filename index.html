Where this is going

Our Story

A family name. An Algonquin business. A reason to keep building.

Chelsea's Kitchen is named after my daughter. It began in Kitigan Zibi with bannock and a simple desire to feed people. Over time, it became something larger: a way to build connection, represent where we come from with pride and create something sustainable for the future.

We call our signature item a Reconciliation Taco because reconciliation does not only happen in institutions. Sometimes it begins when people who may never have spoken end up sharing food.

We are proud Algonquins from Kitigan Zibi, building a sustainable presence in Ottawa.

Rooted in Kitigan Zibi

Sweetened with Awazibi.

Our Maple Berry Bites feature maple syrup made in Kitigan Zibi. It is more than an ingredient. It is part of where we come from and one more way our food carries home with it.
