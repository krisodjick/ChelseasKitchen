Our story

A family name.
A reason to keep building.

Chelsea’s Kitchen is named after Kris Odjick’s daughter, Chelsea. Rooted in Kitigan Zibi Anishinabeg and serving Ottawa, it brings together food, family and community.

Sharing meals in Ottawa helped lead to a growing catering business—and to Restoring Algonquin Presence, a larger initiative creating opportunities for other Algonquin entrepreneurs.
